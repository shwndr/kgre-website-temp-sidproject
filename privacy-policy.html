Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kevin Grimes and the Greatness Real Estate Team ("we," "us," or "our") collect, use, and protect information you provide when you visit KevinGrimesRE.com (the "Site") or contact us through it.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, phone number, and any message details, when you fill out a contact form, schedule a call, or email or call us directly. We may also collect basic technical information (such as browser type and pages visited) through standard website analytics tools.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to your inquiries, schedule showings or consultations, provide real estate services, and communicate with you about properties, market updates, or services you've requested. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Sharing of Information

We may share your information with trusted service providers who help us operate our business (such as our brokerage, Realty ONE Group Connect, or preferred lending partners you choose to be connected with), and only as needed to provide the services you've requested. We do not share your information for third-party marketing without your consent.

Cookies & Analytics

This Site may use cookies or similar technologies to understand site usage and improve the visitor experience. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Your Choices

You may contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to request that we delete it, subject to any recordkeeping requirements under New York real estate law.
